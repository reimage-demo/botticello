Accessibility

Help using our website or requesting service in another way.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Our approach

We want customers to be able to find shop information, request an appointment, and ask for an estimate. Our accessibility goal is to work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. This is a design goal, not a claim of certification or complete conformance.

Using the site

The site includes a skip-to-content link, visible keyboard focus, labeled form fields, text descriptions for meaningful images, and layouts that adapt to smaller screens. You can use Tab and Shift+Tab to move through controls. The mobile menu supports the Escape key to close it. Browser zoom can enlarge text; no accessibility overlay or separate account is required.

Photo uploads are optional. Use the file chooser if dragging images is difficult, or describe the concern in the form without photos. Required fields and validation messages help identify information needed to continue. Phone numbers and the shop address are also available as text.

Get help or report a barrier

If you cannot use a page or form, call 860-953-5622 or write to Botticello Auto Repair, 210 South Street, West Hartford, CT 06110. The shop’s regular hours are Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern time; Saturday and Sunday are closed. You can request service assistance or ask for website information in another format.

When reporting a website problem, include the page address, what you were trying to do, and the difficulty you experienced. Browser or assistive-technology details can help if you choose to provide them. You do not need to disclose a disability or medical information.

Ongoing improvements and outside sites

Accessibility work is ongoing. We have not completed a comprehensive independent accessibility audit, and some barriers may remain. Google Maps, linked reviews, and other external sites are operated by their own providers. If those links prevent you from finding shop information, contact the shop for help.

Learn more from the U.S. Department of Justice’s web accessibility guidance.
