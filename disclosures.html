FTC & consumer disclosures

How to read our reviews, service information, and vehicle listings.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Customer testimonials

The homepage displays five selected excerpts from Google reviews, with links to the original reviews. These are individual customer accounts, not an aggregate rating or a complete collection of feedback. They do not promise a particular repair outcome, price, or turnaround time.

The shop has confirmed that the featured reviewers were not paid or given discounts or free services for their reviews, and are not owners, employees, or relatives connected to the shop. Google is the source platform; displaying a review does not mean Google endorses the shop.

Any future testimonial involving compensation, free or discounted service, or another material connection must identify that relationship beside the testimonial. A general disclosure page is not a substitute for that notice. Read the FTC’s guidance on endorsements and disclosures.

Website provider credit

“Powered by Re-Image Business Solution” identifies the provider of this website. The credit links to Re-Image’s website; it is not a customer review, an independent rating, or a government certification. Service and vehicle inquiries on this site are directed to Botticello Auto Repair.

Estimates and service claims

Online forms collect information for the shop to review. They do not calculate a guaranteed repair price, confirm a diagnosis, authorize work, or confirm an appointment. The shop may need to inspect the vehicle before giving a price. Tow and rental checkboxes are requests to discuss arrangements, not confirmed bookings or a promise of free service.

Warranty coverage, charges, and completion times must be confirmed for the specific work. See our terms of use and Connecticut’s auto repair guidance.

Used vehicle sales

Confirm a listed vehicle’s availability, condition, mileage, total price, and any applicable charges before purchase. Request a vehicle history report and consider an independent inspection. This website does not make a blanket claim that vehicles are defect-free, certified, covered by a particular warranty, or sold “as is.”

For sales covered by the FTC Used Car Rule, the seller must display and provide a vehicle-specific Buyers Guide describing warranty coverage or the applicable warranty status. Ask to read that guide, any separate written warranty, and the purchase agreement before signing. A website disclosure cannot replace those documents or change rights provided by law. See the FTC’s Used Car Rule guidance.

Questions or corrections

If a review, price, or other statement appears inaccurate, contact 860-953-5622 or write to Botticello Auto Repair, 210 South Street, West Hartford, CT 06110. This page explains the website’s disclosures; it does not represent FTC approval or certification.
