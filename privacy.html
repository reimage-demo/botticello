YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy.

Information you share when you request service or an estimate.

Information collected

Our forms collect your name, contact information, vehicle details, service concerns, preferred appointment times, and any photos you choose to upload. Do not include payment card details or sensitive documents in a request.

How it is used

The shop uses this information to review your request, contact you about pricing or service, and manage appointments. Submitting a request does not confirm an appointment or authorize repair work.

Storage and access

Requests and uploaded photos are stored using Convex. Shop administrators use a protected portal to review them. Please include only photos relevant to the vehicle concern.

Questions and requests

Call 860-953-5622 or visit 210 South Street, West Hartford, CT 06110 to ask about information you submitted or request its correction or deletion.
