Privacy policy

What we collect, why we use it, and how to make a privacy request.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Who this notice covers

This notice describes information handled through the Botticello Auto Repair public website and its appointment and estimate forms. Botticello Auto Repair is the business responsible for those requests. Contact us at 860-953-5622 or write to Botticello Auto Repair, 210 South Street, West Hartford, CT 06110 with privacy questions.

Information you provide

Forms collect your name, email address, phone number, vehicle details, service concern, and preferred appointment date or contact method. An estimate request may also include a VIN, mileage, vehicle condition, whether you need a tow or rental, and photos you choose to upload. We also keep the request status, staff notes, and any estimate information added by the shop.

Photos are optional. Upload only images relevant to your vehicle. Files may contain embedded information such as the time or location of a photo; this website does not automatically remove that metadata. Avoid including people, identification documents, payment details, or unrelated personal information.

How information is used

The shop uses request information to understand the vehicle concern, contact you, discuss estimates, arrange service, and manage your request. The system also uses a request identifier and submission limits to prevent duplicate or abusive submissions. Submitting a request is not enrollment in a marketing list or consent to unrelated promotional calls or messages.

Form entries are sent when you submit the request. If a submission or photo upload fails after sending begins, a partial request or uploaded files may already be stored. Contact the shop if you want help locating or deleting a partial submission.

Service providers and access

Convex provides the database and file storage for requests and uploads. Authorized shop administrators access requests through a sign-in-protected portal. Website hosting and technical service providers process information needed to deliver, maintain, and secure the site; authorized technical support may access information when needed to support the service.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to address fraud, security incidents, or legal claims. The public site does not publish customer requests or uploaded photos in testimonials or vehicle listings.

Cookies, fonts, and external links

The public site currently has no advertising pixels or analytics scripts, and does not set application cookies or save form drafts in browser storage. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as an IP address, browser details, and request logs to deliver and protect their services.

Inter fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes requests to Google when loading the site. Maps, Google reviews, and the Re-Image footer credit are external links. Following them takes you to services with their own privacy practices. See Google’s privacy policy and Convex’s privacy policy.

Sale, targeted advertising, and automated decisions

This website has no feature that sells request information, shares it with advertising networks, or uses it for targeted advertising. It does not make automated decisions about credit, insurance, or eligibility for service. An online estimate is reviewed by the shop, not generated as an automatic repair price. There are no sale or targeted-advertising activities in this website for an opt-out preference signal to disable.

Retention and security

The current request system does not automatically delete records after a fixed period. Requests and photos remain stored until removed by the shop. You can ask about the information held for your request and request deletion using the contact details below. Some records may need to be kept for service documentation, legal requirements, or disputes.

The site uses HTTPS connections to its request service and restricts administrative access. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not send payment card details, passwords, or government identification through these forms.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can call or visit instead of using an online form. To request access, correction, a copy, or deletion of information you submitted, contact 860-953-5622 or write to Botticello Auto Repair, 210 South Street, West Hartford, CT 06110. Identify the request using the name and contact information used to submit it. We may need to verify your identity before providing or changing records; do not send sensitive identity documents without first arranging a secure method.

Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights to data portability, to opt out of certain processing, and to appeal a denied request. Where the Connecticut Data Privacy Act applies, a response is due within 45 days, subject to a permitted 45-day extension with notice. To appeal, contact the shop using the same details and identify the decision you want reviewed; covered appeals receive a written response within 60 days. You can also contact the Connecticut Attorney General about privacy rights or complaints.

Children and updates

This site is intended for people seeking vehicle service, not children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact the shop so it can be reviewed and removed as appropriate. Updates to this notice will appear here with a revised date.
